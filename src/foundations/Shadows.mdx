# Shadows

**In Figma:** the Effects on the Foundations page (`shadow/subtle`, `shadow/segment-selected`, `shadow/dialog`, to be
renamed; effect styles have no variable). Right-click a row to see which components use it.

Three, named by elevation: what **rests** on a surface (`raised`), the same a step stronger (`raised-strong`), and what
**floats** over the page (`overlay`). A layer that sits on top also takes its z-index from `--gv-layer-*` (Focus and
states).
