# Voice and tone

The back office is a tool people use for hours, often in a language that isn't their first. The words should get out
of the way: short, plain, and the same every time. The platform's own words are on the
[Word list](?path=/docs/foundations-word-list--docs).

## Voice

- **Plain.** Short words, short sentences. Write "use", not "utilise"; "about", not "regarding".
- **Direct.** Talk to the person: "You can change this later". Say what will happen, not how it works inside.
- **Calm.** No exclamation marks, no jokes in errors, no blame. Something went wrong; say what and what to do.
- **Specific.** Name the thing: "Delete this phase?", not "Are you sure?".

## Sentence case, always

Headings, buttons, menu items, tabs, labels and column headers start with a capital and carry on in lower case:
"Add a phase", "Last active", "Who can take part". Only names keep their capitals: Go Vocal, a page the back office
names (Input manager, Community monitor), a person's name. The copy check flags Title Case headings in docs pages.

## Buttons and links

- **A button starts with a verb** and says what happens: "Publish project", "Add phase", "Send test email". One or two
  words when the context is clear ("Save", "Cancel"), never a sentence.
- **The same action, the same word** everywhere: Delete for gone for good, Remove for taking out of a group (see the
  Word list).
- **A link says where it goes**, so it still makes sense read on its own: "Open the Input manager", not a bare "here".
- **The main action goes last** in a dialog's footer, Cancel before it.

## Errors

An error says what is wrong and how to fix it, in the field's own words, without blame:

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Invalid input | Give the project a title. |
| Error 500 | The ideas didn't load. Check your connection and try again. |
| Field required | Pick a folder. |

Put the message next to what it is about: under the field, in the panel that failed. A form moves focus to the first
field to fix.

## Empty states

An empty state says what will be here and how it gets here, and offers the one next step if there is one: "No inputs
yet" over "Inputs appear here when residents take part.". Tell nothing and no-results apart: "No ideas match these
filters" with a way to clear them.

## Confirmations

- **Before**, for what can't be undone: ask with the thing's name and say what follows. "Delete this user? This can't be
  undone." The button repeats the verb: "Delete", not "OK".
- **After**, a toast in the past tense, with Undo where it can: "Idea archived". No adverb that says it worked
  (the Word list has the one to avoid): the toast already says it.

## Numbers, dates and lists

Never write a number or a date by hand. The parts write them the way the reader's locale does (1,240 in English,
1.240 in Dutch; 30 Oct 2026 or 30/10/2026), from `AppProvider`'s `locale`. A count in a sentence takes the number as
the locale writes it, and the app's plural rules pick the words.

## Every word arrives as a prop

A part never translates and never builds a sentence out of pieces. Every word it shows comes from the page: its content
as props (`title`, `label`, `placeholder`) and its own small words (Close, Next, "Loading more…") as `labels`, with
English defaults (`docs/API.md`, rule 11). So:

- **Never stitch words together.** "3 " + "ideas" + " selected" breaks in every language with another word order or
  other plurals. Hand the part a whole phrase, or a function of the count: `(count, formatted) => string`.
- **Don't put words in CSS or icons.** A word drawn by `::before` or baked into a picture can't be translated.
- **Leave room.** German and Dutch run about a third longer than English; Arabic runs right to left. No fixed widths
  around words.
