# Spacing

**In Figma:** the `Tokens` collection, group `space/`, drawn on the Foundations page's Spacing frame. `space/40` and
`space/64` are Not in Figma yet.

Gaps, paddings and margins, one `--gv-space-*` per value, on a base of 4:
**0 · 2 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 28 · 32 · 40 · 48 · 56 · 64 · 104**. A value off that ladder is no
token: `tests/tokens-only.test.mjs` refuses it and names the steps.

## Which token for which job

The space tokens are the scale itself: there is no second, named tier. Pick by the job, most used first.

{/* A table goes through the Markdown block: MDX here has no GitHub tables. */}
<Markdown>{[
  "| The job | Token |",
  "|---|---|",
  "| Between things that belong together: an icon and its label, a row's parts, a button group | `--gv-space-8` |",
  "| A card's or panel's padding; between cards; between sections inside a panel | `--gv-space-16` |",
  "| A control's inline padding; a compact card's padding; between fields in a form | `--gv-space-12` |",
  "| Inside a control (a chip's padding, an icon to its count); tight stacks of text | `--gv-space-4`, `--gv-space-6` |",
  "| A panel's or page section's padding; between groups | `--gv-space-24` |",
  "| A nudge inside a small control: a segmented control's track, a badge, a breadcrumb | `--gv-space-2`, `--gv-space-10` |",
  "| Roomier panels and tables, an indent, the space above a section | `--gv-space-20`, `--gv-space-28`, `--gv-space-32` |",
  "| An empty state's room; between page regions; a page header's room | `--gv-space-40`, `--gv-space-48`, `--gv-space-56` |",
  "| The Title field's clear zone | `--gv-space-104` |",
].join('\n')}</Markdown>

`--gv-space-64` is the next step on the ladder and unused so far; `--gv-space-0` is no gap.

A 1px or 3px offset is not spacing: on a position (`top`, `left`, `inset`, `translate`, a `margin` that nudges a mark
onto a line) write it raw. Padding, gap and radius never take one.

## Snapped to base 4

The steps code had that base 4 dropped, and where their uses went (`npm run tokens:rename`): 5 → 4, 7 → 8, 9 → 8,
11 → 12, 14 → 16, 15 → 16, 18 → 16, 22 → 24, 25 → 24, 44 → 40. A 1 or 3 stays raw on a position and takes 2 or 4
anywhere else.
