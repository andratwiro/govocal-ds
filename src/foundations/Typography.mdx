# Typography

Nine type roles from the Figma Foundations page, on the 12 · 13 · 14 · 16 · 20 · 28 scale, Regular and Medium only:
hierarchy comes from size steps and grey versus black, not from extra weights. A component sets a role with
`font: var(--gv-type-<role>)` and `letter-spacing: var(--gv-type-<role>-tracking)`.
