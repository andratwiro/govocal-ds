# Focus and states

**In Figma:** the stroke widths are the `Tokens` collection's `border/width-1` and `border/width-2`; the focus ring,
opacity and layers are **Not in Figma** yet, written by hand in `tokens/semantic.tokens.json`.

## Focus ring

Every focusable thing draws the same ring on `[data-focus-visible]`: `outline: var(--gv-focus-ring)`, outside the box
with `--gv-focus-ring-offset`, or inside it with `--gv-focus-ring-offset-inset` for rows, tabs and cells that touch
their neighbours. A hand-drawn ring fails `tests/tokens-only.test.mjs`. On a dark fill, where the navy would vanish,
the ring keeps its width and takes a light colour: `var(--gv-border-width-2) solid var(--gv-border-inverse)` (the side
nav has its own, `--gv-side-nav-border-focused`).

## Opacity

Anything that can't be used is drawn at 45%; a dragged row at 60%; what steps back while another series is picked out
at 40%. Any other opacity carries a `raw:` note saying why.
