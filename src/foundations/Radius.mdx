# Radius

**In Figma:** the `Tokens` collection, group `radius/`, drawn on the Foundations page's Radius frame.

Corner radii, one `--gv-radius-*` per value: **2 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 12 · 14 · 22 · full**.

## Which radius

Most used first:

- **8**: controls: buttons, fields, selects, menus and their items.
- **full**: pills, avatars, dots, switches.
- **4**: small marks inside a control or a chart: a checkbox, a tag, a bar, a tooltip.
- **12**: cards, panels, popovers, banners and toasts.
- **6**: rows and small controls: list rows, breadcrumbs, a segmented control, chips.
- **10**: an icon badge, a drop zone, a framed block inside a panel.
- **14**: what floats over the page: a modal, a sheet, the side panel.
- **2**: a legend's or a chart's tiny marks; **22**: the phone preview's frame.
